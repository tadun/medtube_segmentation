Adding a depth channel to a compact 2.8\,M-parameter model outperforms a network ten times larger trained on RGB alone---the central finding of this work on automated medical tube segmentation. Using an Intel RealSense D415 depth camera, a dataset of 3\,000 paired RGB and depth images was captured across four tube classes (Universal, Push-on, Screwcap, Other) and annotated using SAM\,3-assisted polygon labelling on Roboflow. Five models were evaluated across RGB, RGBD, and depth-only modalities: YOLO11n-RGBD achieved 0.929 mask mAP\textsubscript{50--95} against 0.905 for the much larger YOLOv8m-seg, whilst YOLO26n-seg delivered the best speed--accuracy trade-off at 23.1\,ms per frame. The depth-only model reached 0.804, confirming that tube geometry alone is discriminative---and inherently robust to the reflective, transparent surfaces that confuse RGB classifiers. A live inference pipeline renders segmentation masks over paired RGB and depth streams at 30\,fps, demonstrating that accurate, edge-deployable tube classification is achievable with compact models suitable for clinical waste-sorting deployment.
