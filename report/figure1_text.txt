FIGURE 1 — END-TO-END PROJECT PIPELINE
=======================================

STAGE 1: Data Collection
Hardware: Intel RealSense D415 stereo depth camera, ring-light illumination,
          matte black background, USB 3.2 to Apple M1 Max workstation.
Protocol: 30 capture sessions, one tube per session, 100 frames each.
Output:   3,000 aligned RGB + 16-bit depth image pairs at 1280×720, 30 fps.
Notes:    Tubes placed individually (matching the inclined-conveyor single-tube
          presentation of the target deployment). Auto-exposure disabled;
          depth post-processed with spatial, temporal and hole-fill filters.

↓

STAGE 2: Data Annotation
Platform: Roboflow (v4.0) — upload, versioning, split management, export.
Method:   SAM 2 (Segment Anything Model v2) click-based auto-segmentation
          followed by manual polygon refinement of cap boundaries.
Classes:  4 cap-closure types — Other, Push-on, Screwcap, Universal.
Scale:    3,000 images annotated; 15 images annotated from scratch where
          SAM 2 failed. Dataset split 70 / 15 / 15 (train / val / test).

↓

STAGE 3: Model Training
Models:   5 YOLO-family instance segmentation models compared.
Inputs:   RGB (3-channel), RGBD (4-channel, depth appended), Depth-only (1-channel).
Hardware: M1 Max CPU and Roboflow cloud GPU.
Config:   100 epochs, image size 640×640, cosine LR schedule,
          aggressive augmentation (mosaic, 180° rotation).
Best:     YOLO11n-RGBD — mask mAP50-95 = 0.929 (2.8 M parameters).

↓

STAGE 4: Model Evaluation
Metrics:  COCO box and mask mAP at IoU 0.50 (mAP50) and 0.50–0.95 (mAP50-95).
Analysis: Per-class precision, recall, confusion matrices, F1 curves.
Speed:    End-to-end inference latency (pre-process + forward + post-process)
          measured on M1 Max CPU, averaged over the 450-image test split.
Key finding: YOLO11n-RGBD (2.8 M params, RGBD) outperforms YOLOv8m-seg
          (27.2 M params, RGB-only) at nearly 5× higher frame rate.

↓

STAGE 5: Live Deployment
Pipeline: YOLO26n-seg running at 30 fps on M1 Max CPU.
Display:  2×2 grid — raw RGB stream, RGB with masks, depth heatmap,
          depth heatmap with masks overlaid.
Features: Runtime model switching (M key), snapshot capture (Space),
          continuous recording at 2 fps, per-class colour coding.
Status:   Validated on static tubes; dynamic belt testing pending conveyor
          hardware delivery. SR2000 scanning head identified for production line.


CAPTION
-------
End-to-end project pipeline from hardware capture through live deployment.
Each stage is illustrated with a representative screenshot. Data are collected
with the Intel RealSense D415 depth camera, annotated on Roboflow using
SAM 2-assisted polygon labelling, and prepared as RGB, RGBD, and depth-only
variants. Five YOLO-family segmentation models are trained and evaluated with
COCO metrics; the best-performing model (YOLO11n-RGBD, mask mAP50-95 = 0.929)
feeds a live inference pipeline.
